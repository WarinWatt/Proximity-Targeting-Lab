## Delivered

- Shared shell on 11 pages with role navigation, simulation/pilot labels, mobile menu and per-page evidence reports.
- Ten advanced simulation scenarios; reservations include the maximum engagement premium and expose budget snapshots.
- Persistent pilot supports five stations, shared campaign budget locks, payload-bound idempotency, append-only charges/refunds and retrospective review.

## Wallet and separated workspace update

- New service entry portal.html separates Ads Owner, System Owner, Station Operator/Caster and Live Monitor; research navigation is collapsed separately. Login redirects use role-specific allowlists.
- Grant-credit wallet, append-only wallet ledger, prefunded purchases, explicit legacy campaign funding, close/release and refunds after closure are deployed. External top-ups remain unimplemented.
- Initial 1,000 THB-denominated grant issued once to lab-customer under a fixed, payload-bound key. This is advertising credit, not a bank receipt.
- Caster can preview its local camera and play verified cached clips in its own browser. It does not cast to a remote TV agent. Live Monitor polls API state every ten seconds; no remote camera feed or trusted device heartbeat is claimed.
- 46 unit tests and 11 independent wallet HTTP checks pass, including concurrent purchase affordability, idempotency, tenant isolation, refunds both before/after close, legacy funding and immutable ledger.
- Browser acceptance with a generated playable WebM passed owner grant → customer purchase → prefetch/checksum → auction → playback completion. Public HTTPS role entrypoints, wallet credit, caster and monitor were verified. Mobile portal and advertiser layouts were visually inspected; release-versioned CSS avoids stale CDN styles.
- Per-page evidence registers and ERD include wallet entities and relationships. Deployment backup is retained privately under .artifacts/releases/20261115-wallet-portals.

This release is an internet-accessible research pilot, not approved monetary production. Campaign Sales remains a simulation; persistent purchasing is in the station pilot page. Targets are simulated, browser playback reports are not trusted device attestations, attention is manually entered, and no real payment provider is integrated. OpenPAR inference accuracy, real gaze validity, operational load/power measurements, offline fleet synchronization, annotation workflow and dataset/model lifecycle still require implementation and validation.
